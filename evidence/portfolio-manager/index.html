Evidence & Methodology

Autonomous Portfolio Manager (private)

Market-calibrated LLM agents reason over financials, web search, and filings retrieval to generate investment theses, executing autonomously through Alpaca behind risk guardrails and circuit breakers. Every model rating is snapshotted with its evidence and joined to a realized forward return.

All figures on this page share one endpoint: 2026-07-31. The headline moves roughly ±1 percentage point per trading day, so a figure quoted without its date is meaningless.

This is walk-forward paper trading, not a live-money track record. The distinction is not cosmetic and is not buried further down the page.

02

Three Questions A Sharp Reader Will Ask

Answered here before anyone has to ask them.

Q1Paper or real money?

Paper. The paper account is the committed source of truth for every figure above. A real-cash mirror has run since 2026-07-13 under a hard cap, but the headline is never presented as a live-money track record.

What makes it meaningful anyway is that it is walk-forward: every decision was made in real time, before its outcome was known, and nothing was ever retroactively fitted to realized outcomes. That is a stronger claim than a backtest, which is why it is the framing used throughout.

It is not a claim that nothing is ever re-fit. The candidate ranker refits on each run — standard walk-forward practice — and the judge prompt was revised mid-period on 2026-07-11. Both are forward-only; neither looks at an outcome it then gets scored on.

Q2How many independent observations?

Fewer than the day count suggests. The period contains roughly 10 independent 21-trading-day windows — a small sample, and the honest denominator for any claim made here.

The autocorrelation-robust confidence interval on mean 21-trading-day excess return is [+0.06%, +3.82%].

Positive — but the lower bound barely clears zero. That is the single weakest number on this page, and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to derive.

All such inference routes through one guarded helper precisely because naive standard errors understate uncertainty by roughly 3× on overlapping windows. Getting this wrong is the most common way a strategy result is oversold.

Q3Is it really the LLM, or just beta?

Fair question, and it was measured both ways.

The LightGBM candidate ranker's apparent edge was ~entirely a volatility tilt — partial information coefficient ≈ 0 after controlling for 63-day volatility. It was not picking winners; it was picking volatile names in a rising market.

The LLM judges' signal survives the same control (+0.232 → +0.214; +0.210 → +0.201). So the agents carry the result, not the ranker — which is why the agents lead every description of this system.

That negative result was found on my own work, written up internally rather than quietly dropped, and is repeated here for the same reason.

A superseded writeup exists. An earlier Medium post (16 March 2026) describes the ranker-led version of this architecture. It is a dated snapshot that predates the volatility-tilt finding above, and it is not the source for any figure on this page. Read it as history, not as a current description.
03

What The Evaluation Measures

Every model decision is snapshotted with the evidence it was based on and joined to a realized forward return. That produces per-model skill — rank information coefficient with date-clustered confidence intervals, calibration, and a null-anchored rating board — rather than an impression of which model seems good.

Roster membership is a consequence of that measurement. A model earns its place against a codified statistical bar and loses it the same way; no model is included because of its brand.

04

Measurement Bugs, Found And Corrected

Both were documented rather than quietly patched. A performance number is only as good as the measurement code behind it.

A horizon convention that silently measured the wrong window

The evaluation horizon was labelled 21 trading days everywhere but actually measured 14. Correcting it reversed a live model-deprecation decision — a model had been dropped on the strength of a number that was measuring something other than what it claimed.

A fill poller that recorded intermediate partial quantities

The broker fill poller captured partial quantities mid-execution, corrupting 207 ledger rows. It was root-caused, fixed at the source rather than patched downstream, and the affected history was restated from broker truth. The figures published on this page reflect that restatement.

05

Safety Architecture

Trades execute autonomously — no human approves an individual order. Oversight is structural rather than manual: read-only dashboards, automatic halts, and a kill switch.

  • Execution circuit breakers reject a defective target before any order reaches the broker.
  • Decision and execution are decoupled workflows, so a fault in one cannot silently drive the other.
  • The real-cash mirror is hard-capped and independently monitored.
  • Invariants are test-enforced — for example, shadow evaluation can never influence the live book.
  • A scheduled agent audits the project's own validation calendar, proposes corrections, and is firewalled from editing it. A frozen 7-case evaluation measures whether the model doing that job is actually any good.
